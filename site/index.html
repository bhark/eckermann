This is why modern man has heard enough about guilt and sin. He is sorely beset by his own bad conscience, and wants rather to know how he is to reconcile himself with his own nature; how he is to love the enemy in his own heart, and call the wolf his brother.

  1. Press Ctrl Alt Space Once to start, once more to stop.
  2. Speak naturally The orb listens and shows what it hears, punctuation and all.
  3. It writes for you Each phrase lands at your cursor, in any app, while you keep talking.
